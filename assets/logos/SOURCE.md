# Airline logos

Downloaded by `tools/fetch_logos.py` from [Jxck-S/airline-logos](https://github.com/Jxck-S/airline-logos)
at commit `e0b218991caef4832e0db911136173e8eead942a`: the `flightaware_logos` set, with `radarbox_logos` for
codes it lacks. Files are named by ICAO airline code.

The logos are trademarks of their airlines. Contrail shows them only to identify the airline of a flight
overhead. The source repository carries no licence of its own.

`tools/build_pack.py --logos assets/logos --out logos.pak` turns this folder into the SD card pack.
