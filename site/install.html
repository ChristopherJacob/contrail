Setup

  1. Plug the display in with a USB data cable (some cables only charge). On the two-port board either port works.
  2. Click Install and pick the board's serial port (often “USB Serial”, “CH340” or “CP210x”). The installer asks whether to erase the device; leave it off to keep your settings when updating, or turn it on for a clean start. Installing takes about a minute.
  3. Join the Contrail-setup Wi-Fi network from a phone or computer when the display asks. Pick your home Wi-Fi and enter its password.
  4. Set your location (latitude and longitude) on the setup page, or leave it empty and Contrail finds roughly where you are from your internet connection. After that, the board's web page at http://contrail.local has every setting.
  5. Optional: an SD card. For real airline logos and a detailed Live Map, build the SD card for your home in the browser and copy it onto a FAT32 microSD card.
