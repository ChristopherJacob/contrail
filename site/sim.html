Simulator

The real Contrail firmware, compiled to WebAssembly and running in your browser, on flights recorded over a handful of airports in the last hour. Tap and swipe the screen as you would the board.

Theme
Screen

Swipe sideways to change screens, press and hold for the quick settings, and tap a plane to select it. Ready for the real thing? Install Contrail on a board.

The simulator needs a browser with Web Workers and WebAssembly, such as a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
